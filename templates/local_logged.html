Health Awareness
Common water-borne diseases
  • Cholera - severe diarrhea and dehydration
  • Typhoid - high fever, weakness, stomach pain
  • Hepatitis A - jaundice, fatigue, abdominal pain
  • Dysentery - bloody diarrhea and fever
Prevention tips
  • Always drink boiled or filtered water
  • Wash hands frequently with soap
  • Keep water storage containers clean
  • Report water quality issues immediately
