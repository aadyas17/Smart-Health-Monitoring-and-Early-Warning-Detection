Hygiene Instructions & Prevention Tips
Hand Hygiene

Wash hands with soap for at least 20 seconds, especially before eating and after using the toilet.

Water Safety

Always drink boiled or filtered water. Store water in clean, covered containers. Avoid unknown sources.

Food Safety

Eat freshly cooked food. Wash fruits and vegetables thoroughly. Avoid street food during outbreak periods.

Sanitation

Use proper toilets. Keep surroundings clean. Dispose of waste correctly to prevent contamination.

Disease Prevention

Get vaccinated. Seek medical attention immediately if symptoms like diarrhea or fever develop.

Community Awareness

Report water quality issues early. Share hygiene knowledge with neighbors and family members.

Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about the system.

This is an AI-based water-borne disease surveillance and early warning system developed by the Department of Information Science, Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering. It uses machine learning to analyze water quality data and predict disease outbreak risks, serving citizens, ASHA workers, and health officials.

Three types of users: Local Citizens who report water quality issues and health symptoms; ASHA Workers who submit field data and receive AI predictions; and Health Officials who monitor trends and manage the system through the admin dashboard.

The system achieves 85–92% accuracy using Logistic Regression and Support Vector Machine (SVM) models. The F1 score ranges from 0.80 to 0.90, making it reliable for early warning and prevention.

Register or login through the Local Citizen Portal, then fill out the complaint form with your location, observed symptoms, and water quality issues. Your report will be forwarded to local health officials for action.
